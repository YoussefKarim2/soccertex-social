# Trending sounds — what's real, and what you can legally do

Live data pulled **8 November 2026** from tokchart.com (worldwide TikTok audio chart,
updated 05:00 UTC daily).

---

## The hard part first

**I cannot download a trending sound and put it in your videos.** Not won't — can't, legally.

Trending audio on TikTok and Instagram is licensed **by the platform, for use inside their
app**. Their deals with rights holders cover a user tapping a sound in the app. They do not
cover anyone downloading the track and muxing it into an MP4 elsewhere.

If I baked a chart track into your videos, three things would happen, in order:

1. The platform's audio fingerprinting would detect it within minutes of upload.
2. Your audio would be muted, or the video removed.
3. On a **business** account the exposure is worse, because it's commercial use — repeat
   detections put a new account at real risk.

A brand-new account getting audio strikes in its first month is the worst possible start.
So the videos ship with music I generated, which nobody owns but you.

**But you can have trending sounds — in the app, at upload, in about five seconds.** That is
the legitimate route, it's free, and it's how every brand account does it.

---

## How to actually do it

TikTok, when you upload:

1. Pick your video → **Next**
2. Tap **Add sound** at the top
3. Search the sound by name, or browse **Trending**
4. Pull the volume slider: **your video's audio down to ~20%, the trending sound up to ~80%**

The videos are built for exactly this. The music bed sits low and the whooshes are short, so
a trending sound drops over the top without fighting it. Burned-in captions mean the video
still reads with your audio muted entirely.

Instagram Reels: same flow — the music note icon during editing.

> **One account setting matters.** TikTok **Business** accounts are restricted to the
> Commercial Music Library and cannot use most chart tracks. **Creator** accounts can.
> This is why `SETUP.md` says choose Creator. If you've already made a Business account,
> switch it before you post.

## My recommendation per video — and a caution

**Most of that list is wrong for you.** Numbers 2–7 are meme formats: a specific sound tied
to a specific joke or transition. A kit manufacturer using them reads as a brand trying to
be young, which is the thing buyers scroll past.

Two are genuinely usable:

| Video | Sound | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ST-V001` lead-time | **Paper Moon Static** (#1) | Biggest reach on the platform; neutral enough not to clash with a business message |
| `ST-V002` decoration | **Paper Moon Static** or **Trendformusic** (#8) | The slowed jersey-club edit suits a satisfying process reveal |
| `ST-V003` GSM | **Piano Hush** (#10) | Calm, lets a teaching voiceover sit on top |
| `ST-V004` quality | **Piano Hush** | Same — a serious subject, don't undercut it |
| `ST-V005` Europe | **keep the generated bed** | This one goes to LinkedIn, where trending audio means nothing |

**The honest caution.** The benchmark research on teamwear accounts found the formats that
actually performed were teaching and comparison reels — @ats_boeckmann getting 6–15K views
on a 20K-follower account with "which garment does which job" and "€36 vs €16". None of that
was trend-chasing. A trending sound raises the ceiling on a video that is already good; it
does not rescue one that isn't.

So: use them, but treat them as a 5% lift on top of a strong hook, not the strategy.

## Chart decay

Sounds move fast — #3 on this list gained **367% in a single day**. By the time you read
this the top five will likely have changed.

**Check it the morning you post**, free, no account:

- `tokchart.com/tiktok-trending-sounds` — worldwide, updated 05:00 UTC
- `tokchart.com/tiktok-trending-sounds/united-states` and `/uk` — your two markets
- TikTok's own **Creative Center → Trends → Songs**, filterable by country
- Or just open TikTok's **Add sound → Trending** tab, which is the same data at the source

Pick anything in the top 10 that doesn't carry an obvious meme format. That's the whole
decision.
